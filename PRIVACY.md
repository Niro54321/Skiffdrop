# Skiffdrop privacy policy

*Last updated: 7 November 2026*

Skiffdrop sends files and text between your own phone and your own Windows PC over
your local network. It is made by Niro54321. It has no account, no ads, no analytics
and no telemetry.

## Nothing goes to the internet

Skiffdrop never connects to the internet. The phone talks only to the PC on your own
network (Wi-Fi or Ethernet): the page the phone opens is served by your PC, and the
files and text go straight from one device to the other. Skiffdrop refuses
connections from addresses outside your private network.

## What is kept on your PC

- **Files you receive** are saved in the folder you choose (by default
  `Downloads\Skiffdrop`). Skiffdrop never deletes them.
- **Settings, the phones you allowed and the history** of what was sent and received
  (file names, sizes, times, and the text of messages) are kept in Skiffdrop's data
  folder (`%APPDATA%\Skiffdrop`, or the app's private folder when installed from the
  Microsoft Store). Clearing a list in Skiffdrop removes it from the history.
  Uninstalling Skiffdrop removes this folder.
- For each phone you allowed, Skiffdrop stores its name (for example
  "iPhone · Safari"), its local network address and a fingerprint of a random key.
  The key itself stays only on the phone, in a browser cookie.

## What is kept on the phone

Only that cookie (so it doesn't have to pair again) and your language choice in the
browser's storage. Files you save on the phone go where you save them.

## Children

Skiffdrop does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
